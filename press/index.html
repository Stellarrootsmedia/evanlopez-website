Short bio

Evan Lopez is a stand-up comedian based in Austin, Texas. He was featured on OFTV's most-watched episode of the "LMAOF" stand-up series, has millions of views across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, hosts the weekly podcast Seven Minutes in Evan, and founded the Austin production company Top Flight Comedy.

Long bio

Based in Austin, Texas, Evan Lopez has performed at the top comedy clubs in town, from the historic Creek and the Cave and the Comedy Mothership to Austin favorite Cap City Comedy Club, Sunset Strip Comedy Club and the underground Black Rabbit ATX. He's a regular at East Austin Comedy Club.

Evan was featured on OFTV's most-watched episode of the "LMAOF" stand-up specials. Online, his bird-watching series and short comedy videos have pulled in millions of views across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, with a growing flock of people who now know more about cardinals than they ever planned to.

He was born in a small desert town in California to a mixed household. Growing up half white, half Puerto Rican, and a feral homeschool kid gave him the unusual upbringing he brings to the stage.

Evan believes laughter is the best medicine. Unless you have good insurance.
